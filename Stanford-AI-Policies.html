How to read this page

Lenses are the five roles faculty work in. Dimensions are where risk enters: what goes in (governed by data classification and privacy), the context that grounds the output (governed by licenses and copyright), and what comes out (governed by disclosure and accountability).

Each card pairs capabilities (the tools available) with limitations (the policies and published guidance that apply, and who authored them). Its cases are risk chains: what can go wrong → who bears it → when it shows up → what limits it → who authored it.

Who bears it. Self: your reasoning, reputation, credibility, IP and liability. Others: patients, learners, colleagues, participants, and the shared record (the literature, peer review). Institution: Stanford's licenses, data, compliance and standing.

Case status. Clear policy and owner · Partial, unpublished, or contested · No clear policy or owner today.

Evidence badges. Required a rule in force defines the harm · Evidence-based studies measure it · Prudent default plausible, untested · Unverified not yet confirmed from the primary source.

Policy list colors. A red edge marks a rule in force; an orange edge marks guidance.

Navigating. The arrow keys (or space) move from the cube to each role, then across its Data In, Context and Data Out cards, then back out to the role and to the cube, which turns to the next role. Click a case to open it; inside a case the arrows step through it and return to the card after the last step. Esc goes up one level.

Reasoning is not a separate dimension. Its risk depends on role and level: learners risk never building a skill; experienced clinicians are not protected from automation bias.

Guidance by Stanford authors is labelled as such in step 4. It is advice, not policy.

Links. Policy names link to the issuing body's page; guidance links to the published article. Study citations link to the DOI or publisher page. Policies change; check the date on the linked page before you rely on it. Lane Guidance is not yet published, so it is not linked.
